Bangladeshi Public Holidays (fixed dates)

    Holiday markers also appear inside the month grid on the matching Gregorian date. Religious holidays (Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Adha, Durga Puja and others) depend on the lunar calendar / official gazette and change every year, so only fixed Gregorian holidays are listed here. Conversion follows the standard Bangladesh rules: Boishakh–Bhadro 31 days, Ashwin–Choitro 30 days, Falgun 29 days (30 in a leap year) — with Agrahayan at 31 days in the official Bangladesh calendar, which is what makes the year total 365/366 days and keeps 1 Boishakh on 14 April every year.
